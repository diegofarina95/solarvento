Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This policy explains what data SolarVento (hereinafter, "the site", accessible at solarvento.es) processes when you use the solar self-consumption calculator, for what purpose, on what legal basis and what rights you have. SolarVento is a free tool that estimates solar panels, batteries and savings from your electricity bill, with no registration or account creation.

1. Data controller

Controller: SolarVento. For any query about privacy or to exercise your rights you can write to diegofarina95@gmail.com.

2. What data we process and for what purpose

  • Your electricity bill. When you upload a bill, it is processed to extract consumption data (kWh, periods, power, etc.) and estimate your installation. If the bill is a PDF with text, that text is anonymised before being sent to the artificial intelligence provider that performs the extraction: first and last names, DNI/NIF/NIE/CIF and IBAN are removed. If it is an image or a scanned PDF (with no text layer), before it is sent, optical character recognition (OCR) is applied on our own server to mask that same data directly on the image itself. This masking is automatic and may not be perfect (a piece of data the OCR fails to read, or an unlabelled name, might not be masked; and if the OCR were unavailable, the image would be sent as is), so the image that is sent may still include some of that data; it is used solely to extract the consumption. Under no circumstances is the original file stored permanently.
  • Extraction result. The result is stored temporarily in a cache linked to a session cookie in your browser, so as not to repeat the analysis of the same bill. These entries are deleted when the browser is closed and, at most, after 24 hours.
  • Location. The location you indicate (address or point on the map) is used to query the solar radiation in the European Commission's PVGIS service. We do not associate it with your identity.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is used in a limited way to prevent abuse and apply bill upload limits (service security).

3. Cookies

  • Necessary session cookie (solvento_bill_session): allows the bills you analyse to be associated with your session. It has no expiry date: it disappears when the browser is closed.
  • Third-party advertising cookies (Google AdSense): used to display ads. They are only activated if you give your consent. By default, advertising cookies are denied (Consent Mode) until you accept in the consent message.

4. Google advertising

This site uses Google AdSense, a Google advertising service. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to display ads based on your visits to this and other sites, provided that you have given your consent. You can see how Google uses the information in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services" and manage your preferences in My Ad Center. Consent is collected through a consent management platform (CMP) certified by Google, and you can change your decision at any time from the consent message itself.

5. Third parties and international transfers

To provide the service we rely on these providers:

  • AI provider (OpenAI): receives the anonymised text of the bill to extract the consumption data. This may involve a transfer to the USA covered by the safeguards provided for under the GDPR.
  • Google (AdSense): advertising, subject to consent.
  • PVGIS (European Commission): solar radiation data based on the location.

6. Legal basis

  • Performance of the service you request (art. 6.1.b GDPR): processing your bill and calculating the estimate.
  • Consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR): cookies and third-party advertising.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR): security and prevention of service abuse.

7. Retention

The bill data and the result of its analysis are kept only during your session (they are deleted when the browser is closed and, at most, after 24 hours). We do not keep your bills or your personal data beyond what is necessary to provide you with the service at that moment.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, as well as to restrict or object to the processing, to data portability and to withdraw your consent at any time. Since the service does not require registration and the bill data is deleted with your session, most of the information ceases to be available when the browser is closed. To exercise your rights, write to diegofarina95@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the service or in the regulations. We will publish the current version on this same page, indicating the date of the last update.
